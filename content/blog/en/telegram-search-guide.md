Telegram is more than a messenger: it runs a whole layer of search-engine bots that find channels, groups, files and answers, each handled by a specialist bot.

## Three kinds of bots

- **Network search**: finds channels and groups across Telegram. Throw in keywords, get heat-ranked results.
- **File retrieval**: finds documents, ebooks and installers. Stick to reputable veteran bots; do not add every newcomer.
- **Q&A**: bots wired to large models, usable as a search engine when you want a one-line answer.

## Find it in three steps

1. Decide whether you want a channel, a file or an answer, then pick the right category.
2. Rotate two or three keywords. If one search fails, rephrase instead of hammering the same bot.
3. Read the pinned post and bio before joining; check size and format before downloading; cross-check answers with a second bot.

## The avoid list

- Anything asking for payment or prepayment first: block it.
- Anything asking for your verification code or a click on a strange link: that is phishing, not service.
- Anything named after a famous model with no official backing: treat it as an unknown third party.
