Connecting personal WeChat allows you to chat directly with your PersonaBot from your phone. With no public IP or webhook endpoint required, you simply scan a QR code to pair your WeChat account and exchange text, files, images, and voice messages with your Bot.

## Scope and features

- **1:1 Direct Chat Only**: Exclusively supports conversations between your personal WeChat account (the one scanning the QR code) and the paired Bot. Group chats, other contacts, and WeCom are not supported.
- **Rich Media**: Supports text, file transfers (up to 25 MiB), image comprehension and returns, voice transcript processing, and audio playback.
- **Slash Command Controls**: Send commands like `/status`, `/model`, `/effort`, and `/new` directly in WeChat to manage your Bot on the go.
- **Local Credentials**: Pairing tokens and conversation transcripts remain on your local computer.

## 1. Scan the QR code to connect WeChat

1. In DSH, open **Settings → IM bots → WeChat**.
2. Click **Scan to bind** to generate a temporary pairing QR code.
3. Open WeChat on your phone, scan the QR code, and confirm the login prompt.
4. Once connected, your account status will show **Connected / Ready**, and a chat session with the Bot will appear in your mobile WeChat chat list.

> Tip: QR codes are temporary login credentials—never share them publicly. Your paired credentials persist in your local Profile across restarts.

## 2. Bind the WeChat account to your PersonaBot

1. In **Bot mode**, open the target Bot's DM.
2. Expand **External identities** in the right-hand **Channel sidebar** and click **+ Bind app**.
3. Select your connected WeChat account from the dropdown list.
4. The status will update to **Ready**. Messages you send to this Bot in WeChat will now flow directly into its Inbox.

## 3. Test and verify replies

1. In your mobile WeChat app, open the Bot chat and send a test message (e.g., "Hello, please reply WECHAT-OK").
2. In DSH, expand **Bot Inbox** in the Bot's sidebar to confirm the incoming message.
3. Within seconds, your mobile WeChat chat will receive the model-generated reply from your Bot.

## 4. Working with media and files

In addition to plain text, you can exchange media seamlessly:

### Sending and receiving files

- Send any document, spreadsheet, or archive under 25 MiB to the Bot in WeChat.
- The Bot stores the file in its authorized workspace and invokes tools to inspect, summarize, or edit it.
- When generating deliverables, the Bot sends the output file directly back to your WeChat chat.

### Photos and images

- Send images or screenshots to the Bot, optionally accompanied by a caption.
- When paired with a vision-capable model (such as DeepSeek Flash), the Bot analyzes the image content and responds accordingly.
- The Bot can also generate or process images and send them back as native WeChat images.

### Voice messages and transcripts

- Send a voice message by pressing and holding the voice button in WeChat.
- The speech-to-text transcript generated by WeChat is delivered directly to the Bot's Inbox.
- In DSH, you can inspect the transcript, view audio duration, or click "Prepare playback" to listen to the audio.

## 5. Remote slash commands

You can send standard slash commands in WeChat (on a line by themselves) to control your Bot without consuming model reasoning tokens:

| Command | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `/status` | View the Bot's active models, status, and settings |
| `/model` | List available models |
| `/model <name or index>` | Switch the Bot's conversation model |
| `/effort <low\|medium\|high>` | Set the model's reasoning effort level |
| `/new` | Reset the conversation history and start a fresh session while preserving long-term memory |
| `/compact` | Compact session context to free up window space |
| `/stop` | Immediately interrupt active model generation or tool execution |

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| QR code expires | Refresh the QR code in DSH and confirm promptly on your phone. |
| Bot does not reply to messages | Check that local DMs work, the WeChat identity shows **Ready**, and the conversation is not muted or blocked. |
| File upload fails | WeChat limits file uploads to 25 MiB. Split or compress larger files before sending. |
| Image not recognized | Ensure the Bot is configured with a model that supports vision / multimodal input. |
| Disconnected after WeChat relogin | Re-scanning the QR code generates a fresh credential; simply re-confirm the identity binding in DSH. |
