- Homepage hero demo follows the current desktop layout: Control Rail, separate Channel list, Messages/Home and Channel subpanels, Activity, one status glyph per row, three-button approvals, optional wallpaper.
- QQ guide (zh/en): current version scope (1.4.1, DSH 0.2.1-rc.2, Provider 4.32.0-botharness.21), "Bot" wording, the QA helper link on main, and a new "What else you can do" section (sync to a local group, two Bots, results after 5 minutes, images/files/voice and ordinary messages as development candidates).
